Good to Know

Dress code

“Whatever you feel most fabulous in”

Formal

We’d love it if you could lean into earthy tones and natural textures that will feel right at home in the Karoo setting. Think dressed up just enough but still relaxed and true to you. Footwear-wise, this is very much a farm wedding. Vellies, Birkenstocks, or any shoes you can comfortably walk and dance in on natural terrain will serve you well.

If you’re set on heels, just keep in mind that the ground may be uneven. As the sun sets, temperatures tend to drop quite quickly, so please bring a jacket or something warm for the evening. Mostly, we just want you to feel good and be ready to boogie.

Weather

April in the Karoo means warm days and cool tp chilly evenings. We recommend bringing an extra layer for after sundown.

Children

Please email us if you have questions about bringing children.

Where to Stay

We recommend booking early — accommodation in Prince Albert fills up quickly.

Prince Albert is filled with charming places to stay. While there are many lovely options in town (including smaller guesthouses, Airbnbs, and private cottages), we’ve reserved a few favourites to make things as easy as possible.

Our top recommendation is the Swartberg Hotel (part of the Corktree Collection). It’s a beautifully restored heritage hotel in the heart of town, with classic Karoo charm, a great restaurant, and everything is within walking distance. It is in the centre of town and we will have our shuttle collection happening here for the wedding events.

Swartberg Hotel Hotel in town

Other options Airbnbs

If you’d prefer something more private, there are plenty of other options around town from quaint cottages to boutique guesthouses. Thankfully, wherever you choose, everything in Prince Albert is close by.

Getting There

Prince Albert is approximately 4.5–5 hours from Cape Town via the N1 and Huguenot Tunnel. The drive through the Huguenot and Du Toitskloof passes is beautiful — allow extra time to enjoy it.

By car

Recommended. Fuel up in Matjiesfontein or Leeu-Gamka. There are petrol stations in Prince Albert if needed.

Carpooling

If you're travelling from Cape Town and need a lift, let us know and we'll help connect you with others making the journey.
