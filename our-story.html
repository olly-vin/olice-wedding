Alice & Oliver

How we met

Alice and Olly first met in primary school at a music evening — he was there playing the violin. Somewhere between the performances and polite small talk, they were introduced. Olly remembers thinking she was strangely cool — the kind of person who replied “Haga Haga” instead of “Haha.”

What followed was a gentle, slightly misaligned childhood almost-romance: MXit messages, overlapping friendships, school events but timing, even then, had its own ideas. They drifted apart in the natural way teenagers do: different schools, different provinces, different lives unfolding. But never entirely out of orbit. There was always a faint awareness, a name that lingered, a small flicker of butterflies when one’s name appeared on the other’s screen.

Falling in love

Years later, having both flirted more with dance floors than with degrees, fate intervened. They found themselves at a predrinks. This time, something clicked. There was a first kiss, followed by a handful of almosts over the next two years, there were moments that hinted at something more, but never quite settled into place.

And then, once again, life carried on… until lockdown. Out of the blue, Olly reached out and asked Alice what she was doing that weekend. A few days later, they found themselves escaping to The Magaliesburg and an easy laughter-filled weekend followed.. From there, things fell into place. Early dates looked a little different like plant shopping, quiet dinners at home, meeting families a lot sooner than expected but it all felt entirely right. Later, when asked why he chose that moment to reach out, Olly’s answer was simple: He had always had a thing for Alice. He just needed to become the man she deserved first.

The next chapter

And now, we can’t wait to celebrate this next chapter with all of you.
